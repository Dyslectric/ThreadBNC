How to search

  • tailscale headscale: both words, anywhere in the post or comment
  • "self hosted": that exact phrase
  • docker*: words starting with docker
  • caddy OR nginx: either word
  • backup -restic: leave out anything mentioning restic

Words are matched whole, ignoring case and accents. Titles count most, then text, then links.
