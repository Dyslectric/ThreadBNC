Pictures for browsing

The feed shows smaller copies of archived pictures, from posts and from linked articles, made at these widths, so browsing doesn't send them at full size. The archived pictures stay as they are, and open in full from a post. Pictures no wider than a width, GIFs (so they still move) and SVGs are shown as they are. Blank: the default; 0: no copies for that view.

Media defaults

{{ media_form('/storage/media-defaults', saved, env, 'Server setting', can_transcode, media_kinds, transcoded, by_rate) }}

For every community that hasn't chosen its own in its Media tab. The server settings come from the THREADBNC_MEDIA_* environment variables. Saving tries again files the old settings left out, and goes through the files already archived: pictures and videos now over the size they're kept as they are up to are transcoded down in the background, replacing the bigger copy. Files of a kind you stop archiving stay.
